### 9.3. Research Demo App Constraints: How to Measure & Present Metrics

**Wisme’s research demo app has a finite set of pre-recorded journeys. Users cannot keep coming back for new content, so traditional retention metrics are capped by design. Here’s how to credibly measure and present metrics for investors:**

#### A. Completion Rate
- **What to Measure:** % of users who finish all available journeys.
- **Why It’s Powerful:** If 70–90% of users complete all journeys, it signals exceptional engagement and content value (far above industry averages).

#### B. Session Length & Frequency
- **What to Measure:** Average session duration, and how many sessions it takes to complete a journey.
- **Why It’s Powerful:** Shows users are willing to spend meaningful time per session and return to finish content.

#### C. “Would Use Again” & “Would Recommend” (Intent)
- **How to Measure:** In-app survey at the end of the last journey:
  - “If more journeys were available, would you continue using Wisme?” (Yes/No)
  - “How many hours per week would you realistically use Wisme if there was unlimited content?” (Numeric)
  - “Would you recommend Wisme to a friend?” (NPS)
- **Why It’s Powerful:** Demonstrates latent demand and product-market fit, even if content is limited.

#### D. Willingness to Pay
- **How to Measure:** Ask: “Would you pay ₹500/month for unlimited access to Wisme?” (Yes/No/Maybe)
- **Why It’s Powerful:** Shows monetization potential.

#### E. Referral/Word of Mouth Intent
- **How to Measure:** “Have you already told someone about Wisme?” (Yes/No)
- **Why It’s Powerful:** Indicates virality potential.

#### F. Simulated Ongoing Engagement
- **If possible:** Release journeys in “waves” to simulate ongoing engagement and measure return rates.
- **Waitlist/Notify Me:** Track how many users opt in to be notified when new journeys drop—shows real demand.

#### G. Qualitative Feedback
- **Collect open-ended feedback:** What did users like? What would make them come back? What topics do they want next?
