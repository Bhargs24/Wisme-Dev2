## 📊 **Content Production Guidelines**

### **Episode Structure Template**
1. **Hook** (30 seconds): Attention-grabbing opener
2. **Story/Analogy** (2 minutes): Relatable explanation
3. **Core Content** (5 minutes): Main learning material
4. **Real Example** (1.5 minutes): Practical application
5. **Actionable Takeaway** (30 seconds): What to do next

### **Voice & Tone**
- **Conversational**: Friend explaining to friend
- **Energetic**: Engaged, not monotone
- **Confident**: Authority without arrogance
- **Inclusive**: Technical concepts accessible to beginners
- **Encouraging**: Building confidence in learners

### **Technical Standards**
- **Audio Quality**: Professional studio recording
- **Background Music**: Subtle, non-distracting
- **Pacing**: Natural speech rhythm, strategic pauses
- **Length Control**: 8-10 minutes target, +/- 1 minute acceptable
- **Quality Assurance**: Multiple review rounds before release
