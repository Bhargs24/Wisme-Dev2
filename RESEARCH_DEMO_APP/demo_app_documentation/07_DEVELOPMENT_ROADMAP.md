## 🔄 **Risk Mitigation**

### **Technical Risks**
- **Audio Streaming Issues**: Comprehensive testing, CDN backup
- **App Performance**: Early optimization, memory management
- **Platform Compatibility**: Multi-device testing program

### **Content Risks**
- **Production Delays**: Buffer time in schedule, backup talent
- **Quality Inconsistency**: Detailed style guide, review process
- **Technical Accuracy**: Expert review panel, fact-checking

### **Research Risks**
- **Low Participation**: Incentive program, marketing campaign
- **Data Quality Issues**: Validation checks, duplicate prevention
- **Statistical Significance**: Conservative sample size planning

### **Business Risks**
- **Market Response**: Pilot testing, iterative improvement
- **Competition**: Unique positioning, patent consideration
- **Funding**: Phased approach, milestone-based funding

This roadmap provides a clear path from concept to research publication, ensuring both technical excellence and academic rigor while building a strong foundation for Wisme's commercial success.
