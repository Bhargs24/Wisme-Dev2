## 🧭 Demo App Screen Flow & Research Strategy

### 1. Welcome / About This Demo
- **Purpose:** Set expectations. Make it clear this is a research prototype, not the full Wisme app.
- **Sample Text:**
  - "Welcome to the Wisme Research Demo!"
  - "This is a special prototype to help us validate new learning methods. The real Wisme app will be your personal AI coach—smarter, more adaptive, and packed with features."
  - "Your feedback here helps us build the future of learning."
- **UX Notes:**
  - Clean, modern layout. Large logo. Minimal text. Button: "See What’s Next"

### 2. Why This Demo? (About This Demo Screen)
- **Purpose:** Explain the research context and why user participation matters.
- **Sample Text:**
  - "Why are we doing this?"
  - "We’re testing a revolutionary conversational learning method. This demo helps us collect data to prove its effectiveness."
  - "You’re not just a user—you’re a co-creator!"
- **UX Notes:**
  - Use icons/illustrations. Short bullet points. Button: "Preview the Full Vision"

### 3. Full App Vision Screens (Multiple, Not Cramped)
- **Purpose:** Excite users/investors about the future Wisme experience.
- **Screens:**
  - **Meet Your AI Coach:** Animated avatar, personalized greeting, progress highlights.
  - **Personalized Learning:** Visuals showing adaptive content, smart recommendations.
  - **Gamified Feedback:** Demo of interactive, fun feedback (emoji sliders, drag-and-drop, etc.)
  - **Smart Analytics:** How the app learns from you to improve your journey.
- **Label:** Each screen clearly marked as "Full App Vision – Coming Soon"
- **UX Notes:**
  - One concept per screen. Spacious, visually engaging. Minimal, impactful text.

### 4. Onboarding (In Demo & Full App)
- **Purpose:** Collect essential user info for research and future personalization.
- **Strategic Questions:**
  - Age, education, learning goals, preferred learning style, prior experience, etc.
  - Use conversational tone: "Tell us a bit about yourself so we can personalize your journey."
  - Use micro-interactions: dropdowns, sliders, avatars, not just text fields.
- **Timing:**
  - Place onboarding right after the vision screens, before main demo content.
  - Explain why: "This helps us match you with the best learning path."

### 5. Demo Experience / Research Participation
- **Purpose:** Let users try the conversational learning flow and collect research data.
- **Strategic Research Questions:**
  - **Pre-Experience:** Baseline knowledge, learning preferences, expectations.
  - **During Experience:** Real-time engagement (emoji reactions, quick polls, micro-feedback after each segment).
  - **Post-Episode:** Comprehension, enjoyment, preference vs. traditional methods, willingness to pay.
  - **Journey Completion:** Overall satisfaction, learning outcomes, recommendation likelihood.
  - **Follow-up (if possible):** Retention test after 1 week.
- **Placement:**
  - Pre-experience: After onboarding, before first episode.
  - During: Embedded as micro-interactions, not interruptive.
  - Post: At natural breakpoints, with positive framing ("How did you like your journey?").
- **UX Notes:**
  - Use conversational, friendly AI coach voice.
  - Gamify where possible (progress bars, badges, fun animations).
  - Avoid long forms—break into small, interactive steps.

### 6. Thank You / Next Steps
- **Purpose:** Close the loop, reinforce user’s impact, and set expectations.
- **Sample Text:**
  - "Thank you for shaping the future of learning!"
  - "Your insights will help us build the smartest, most personal learning coach ever."
  - "Want to stay updated? [Join our waitlist]"
- **UX Notes:**
  - Positive, celebratory visuals. Option to share feedback or sign up for updates.

## 🛠️ UX & Product Engineering Notes
- **Spacing & Layout:** Each screen should feel open and uncluttered. Use whitespace, large touch targets, and clear visual hierarchy.
- **Visuals:** Use modern illustrations, animated avatars, and progress visualizations.
- **Voice & Tone:** Friendly, encouraging, and conversational—never clinical or form-like.
- **Transparency:** Always clarify what’s demo vs. full app. Use banners or notes: "This is a research prototype."
- **Data Quality:** Place research questions at moments of high engagement, use positive framing, and keep interactions short and fun.
- **Onboarding:** Make it feel like the start of a journey, not a barrier.
- **Feedback:** Use micro-feedback and gamification to maximize honest, high-quality responses.
